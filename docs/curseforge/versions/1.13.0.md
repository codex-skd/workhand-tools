<h1>Workhand Tools 1.13.0</h1>

<h2>&#128214; Chunk Anchor &amp; Anchor Tome — Keep a Chunk Loaded Anywhere</h2>
<p>A brand new pair of items, separate from the tool line: a pedestal and a tome that together force-load a chunk permanently, with no player needed nearby.</p>

<hr>

<h3>How it works</h3>
<p>Place the <strong>Chunk Anchor</strong> pedestal on the ground and right-click it with an <strong>Anchor Tome</strong> in hand to insert it &mdash; the chunk it stands in gets force-loaded immediately, the same mechanism behind vanilla's <code>/forceload</code> command, and stays loaded permanently while the tome is inserted. Right-click the pedestal empty-handed (or while sneaking) to extract the tome and release the chunk. Breaking the pedestal while a tome is inserted drops both items and releases the chunk right away &mdash; no orphaned tickets left behind, and the ticket is reconciled automatically on world load so it survives server restarts.</p>

<hr>

<h3>Chunk border indicator</h3>
<p>While a tome is inserted, the four vertical corners of the loaded chunk are outlined in white so it's obvious at a glance which area is force-loaded. When it shows up is configurable in the mod config (<code>chunkAnchorBorderMode</code>):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Always</strong> (default) &mdash; visible within render distance.</li>
<li><strong>Sneak + Looking</strong> &mdash; only while sneaking and looking toward the anchor, within 20 blocks.</li>
<li><strong>Nearby</strong> &mdash; within 20 blocks, regardless of stance or where you're looking.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>A tome that actually feels alive</h3>
<p>The inserted Anchor Tome floats open above the pedestal, spinning slowly and turning its pages back and forth &mdash; the same animation technique behind the book on vanilla's Enchanting Table &mdash; while green <code>enchant</code> particles orbit gently around it.</p>

<hr>

<blockquote>The Chunk Anchor's pedestal model/texture and the Anchor Tome's item texture are adapted, with permission, from klikli-dev's <strong>Occultism</strong> mod.</blockquote>
